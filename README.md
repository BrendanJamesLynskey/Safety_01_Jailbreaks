# Safety 01 — Jailbreaks: Landscape, Techniques &amp; Academic Attacks

A visual engineering deck covering the full jailbreak attack surface: the threat landscape, persona/role-play attacks (DAN, Grandma Exploit), encoding tricks (base64, ROT13, ASCII smuggling, Unicode homoglyphs), multi-turn manipulation, prompt injection through indirect channels, prefix/scaffolded injection, and the three major academic optimisation-based attacks — PAIR, GCG, and AutoDAN — plus why models with RLHF, Constitutional AI, and adversarial training resist better.
